Round and scoring reference

Lower scores are better. Each heart is +1, Q♠ is +13, and J♦ is −10. Charging doubles those values. Taking 10♣ doubles the hand’s score (quadruples it when charged). Shooting all 13 hearts and Q♠ reverses those penalties. A 9 in the led suit extends a trick to eight cards. Money compares your score with the other players’ scores.
